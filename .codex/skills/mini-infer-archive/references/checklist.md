- [ ] `本地资料/里程碑总结/` has the phase summary
- [ ] `本地资料/实验记录/` has the benchmark or profiling record
- [ ] `本地资料/博客草稿/` has at least one draft when the phase called for it
- [ ] `本地资料/开发日志/` has a dated dev log
- [ ] `本地资料/知识笔记/` has a concise note for the phase
- [ ] `本地资料/知识专题/` has at least one deeper topic when the phase introduced a major concept

- [ ] `本地资料/Claude计划/` reflects scope or priority changes when they happened

- [ ] Benchmark and profiling scripts are at least config-valid in dry-run or constructor-level checks
